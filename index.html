How to play iBlokYou

1
Start at your corner.

Each player has 21 tiles. Your first tile must cover your designated corner. On later turns, your tile must touch at least one of your own tiles diagonally, but never along an edge. Opponents’ colours may touch edges.

2
Expand or pass.

Choose, rotate or flip a tile and snap it to the 20×20 grid. If you cannot legally place any tile, pass; you cannot move again that round. The game ends when everyone is unable to place.

3
Negotiate with Power Cards.

Use Force Keep, Swap, Touch Me Not, Face-Saving Passage, or The Mediator’s Bridge when their conditions apply. Explain your choice and reflect on the conflict-management concept.

Scoring: Remaining unit squares count as penalty points. Lowest net score wins. All 21 pieces placed earns +15 bonus; if the final piece is the single square, gain another +5. Politeness Tokens deduct 3 penalty points each.

Some card edge cases are interpreted in this prototype; review the card panel and confirm these interpretations with your course instructor before assessed play.
